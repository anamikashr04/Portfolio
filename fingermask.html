Interactive experiment

Make your own Comic

Frame your live camera view with your fingers and watch yourself turn into a comic panel. Pick a style, add the sound, take your picture.

How it works

Five looks

American comic, anime, Ghibli-inspired, black-and-white manga and pop art. Drag the intensity up for bolder ink.

Your fingers are the frame

Two hands make a four-point frame. One hand makes a pinch lens. Or drag to draw a frame.

Good to know

A live prototype, not tested across devices. The styles are inspired looks, not official art from any studio.

Before the camera turns on

  1. Your browser will ask. The camera stays off until you choose Allow in your browser's own prompt. You can say no.
  2. Video stays on your device. The picture is processed in this page. The code on this page does not upload or send your video or any frame of it. A picture is made only if you press Take photo, and it stays on your device.
  3. One download. To find your hands, the page downloads a hand-tracking model from the public CDN cdn.jsdelivr.net. That is a download to you. Your video is not part of it.
  4. You decide when it stops. The camera is switched off when you press Exit, leave this page, or switch to another tab.
