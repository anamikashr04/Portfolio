Interactive experiment

Finger-Mask Comic Filter

Hold up your hands and frame part of your own live camera view. Inside the frame, the picture is redrawn as a comic or anime-inspired drawing. Move your fingers and the frame moves with them.

How it works

The idea

Reference: a tutorial video demonstrating a comic-style finger-mask editing effect. This page turns that idea into something you can try yourself, live.

What happens live

  • Your camera view is redrawn by a real-time shader (ink outlines, flat colour, halftone dots).
  • Your hands are tracked in the browser. Two hands make a four-point finger frame. One hand makes a pinch lens.

What is different

The tutorial's look comes from editing recorded footage afterwards. A browser cannot do that live, so this prototype uses a real-time drawing effect instead. It is a prototype and has not been tested across devices.

Before the camera turns on

  1. Your browser will ask. The camera stays off until you choose Allow in your browser's own prompt. You can say no.
  2. Video stays on your device. The picture is processed in this page. The code on this page does not upload, send or save your video or any frame of it.
  3. One download. To find your hands, the page downloads a hand-tracking model from the public CDN cdn.jsdelivr.net. That is a download to you. Your video is not part of it.
  4. You decide when it stops. The camera is switched off when you press Exit, leave this page, or switch to another tab.
